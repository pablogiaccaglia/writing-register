# Decisions

## Should the voice copy how its owner writes?
Rules: scope.purpose, team-messages.plain, team-messages.no-shorthand
Decided: 2026-09-14
Confirmed: 2026-09-21

No. It describes what the owner wants to read. Only short team messages borrow the owner's register, and the owner's note shorthand is never imitated.

## Maximum detail or brevity?
Rules: content.keep-every-fact, content.detail-welcome, register.not-telegraphic, content.no-trivia
Decided: 2026-09-14
Confirmed: 2026-09-21

Keep every fact; cut filler, process history and incidental detail; introduce what stays.

## Results or method first in a research document?
Rules: documentation.results-first, documentation.research-order
Decided: 2026-09-21

A short version of the results first, then the method and its mathematics in full, then the detailed results.
