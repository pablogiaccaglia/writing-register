# Work cards

{#work-cards.distil} A card that reports a piece of work (a session, an experiment, an investigation) distils it. {#work-cards.shape} It says what the work was for and what earlier work it builds on, describes what was done at the level of ideas before the method, and gives what was concluded and what stays open. {#work-cards.wrong-turn refines=journey.current-state} It may give a wrong turn one line when knowing about it saves the next person from repeating it. {#work-cards.numbers refines=content.detail-belongs} A number stays only when removing it would change a conclusion, and counts of files, tests, commits, retries or tokens do not appear. {#work-cards.references refines=references.sourced} It references the tracker cards, files and code it relies on. {#work-cards.opinion} Where it offers a judgement of its own, it marks it as an opinion. {#work-cards.first-person} First person is used only in a note written as the voice's owner. {#work-cards.proposal refines=work-cards.references} A card that proposes a project or a thesis opens with an abstract and a section of ideas, each grounded in the work the team has done and linked to the cards that describe it.
