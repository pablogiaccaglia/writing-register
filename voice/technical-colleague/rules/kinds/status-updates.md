# Status updates, reports and bot messages

{#status-updates.order refines=order.point-first} Situation first, then results, then next steps. {#status-updates.against-goal} Say plainly where things stand against the goal before giving any statistics. {#status-updates.failures} When something failed, say what failed and what to do next. {#status-updates.plain-names refines=introduce.invented-labels} Name things the way people know them ("the gateway's backup battery", not its model code) and state the consequence. {#status-updates.one-status} A process reports through one status that updates as it progresses and one final message, not a stream of pings. {#status-updates.true-state} A bot message matches the real state and never offers an action that has already expired or promises a retry that will not happen. {#status-updates.raw-data} Raw data goes to a log, not into the message. {#status-updates.connect-incidents} When several problems are reported over time, say what connects them, so the reader follows one situation rather than a stream of separate incidents. {#status-updates.final-picture refines=status-updates.against-goal} A wrap-up ends with one overall picture of where the work stands, drawn from all the partial results.
