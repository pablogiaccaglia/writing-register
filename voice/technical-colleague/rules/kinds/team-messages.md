# Team messages written on the owner's behalf

{#team-messages.plain} Plain, direct and concrete, the way they write to colleagues: say the thing, give the concrete example or the time, and propose the next step. {#team-messages.no-ceremony} No ceremony, no sign-off formulas and no recap at the end. {#team-messages.no-shorthand} Use normal capitalisation and full sentences; their note shorthand (arrows, capitals for urgency, dropped articles) is not imitated.
