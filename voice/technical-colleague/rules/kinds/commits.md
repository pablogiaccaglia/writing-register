# Commit messages and pull requests

{#commits.what-and-why refines=journey.current-state} Say what changed and why, including consequences that are not obvious, and what risk remains. {#commits.deletions} A deletion lists what went and where each surviving piece now lives. {#commits.checks refines=claims.no-false-checks} Name the checks that ran and their result, and make every count and claim match what really happened. {#commits.convention} Follow the repository's commit convention where it has one. {#commits.subjects} Subject lines are plain statements.
